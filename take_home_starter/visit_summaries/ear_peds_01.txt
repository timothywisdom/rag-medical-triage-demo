SYNTHETIC VISIT SUMMARY. Fictional patient, for interview use only. Not medical advice.

Patient: Maya T., 4 years old, female, weight 16 kg (visit attended by parent)
Visit type: Virtual video visit, with an otoscope image taken at a partner pharmacy
Reason for visit: Left ear pain and fever (38.5°C) since yesterday. Eating and drinking normally.

Assessment: Left middle ear infection (acute otitis media).

Plan:
- Amoxicillin suspension 400 mg/5 mL: give 9 mL by mouth twice daily for 5 days.
- Shake well before each dose and keep in the fridge.
- Children's acetaminophen (Tylenol) may be given for pain or fever, dosed by weight as directed on the package.
- Fever and ear pain usually improve within 48 to 72 hours of starting the antibiotic.

When to seek care:
- Fever still present after 48 to 72 hours on the antibiotic, or swelling or redness behind the ear: seek in-person care the same day.
- Stiff neck, unusual drowsiness or difficulty waking, or signs of dehydration: go to the nearest emergency department.
