SYNTHETIC VISIT SUMMARY. Fictional patient, for interview use only. Not medical advice.

Patient: Daniel K., 34, male
Visit type: Virtual video visit with positive rapid strep test from a local pharmacy
Reason for visit: Sore throat and fever for 2 days. No cough.

Assessment: Strep throat (streptococcal pharyngitis).

Plan:
- Amoxicillin 500 mg by mouth twice daily for 10 days.
- Finish the full 10-day course even if you feel better sooner.
- You are considered contagious until you have taken the antibiotic for 24 hours and have no fever. You may return to work after that.
- Ibuprofen (Advil) or acetaminophen (Tylenol) may be used for throat pain or fever as directed on the package.
- Replace your toothbrush after 24 hours on the antibiotic.

When to seek care:
- Trouble breathing, unable to swallow your own saliva, drooling, or a muffled voice: call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.
- A new rash, or no improvement after 3 days of antibiotics: book a follow-up visit.
