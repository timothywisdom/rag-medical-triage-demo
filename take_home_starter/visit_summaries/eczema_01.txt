SYNTHETIC VISIT SUMMARY. Fictional patient, for interview use only. Not medical advice.

Patient: Jordan L., 22, non-binary
Visit type: Virtual video visit with photos
Reason for visit: Itchy, dry, red patches in the elbow creases and on the forearms for 1 week. History of eczema since childhood.

Assessment: Eczema flare (atopic dermatitis), arms only. No signs of infection.

Plan:
- Betamethasone valerate 0.1% cream: apply a thin layer to the affected areas on the arms twice daily for up to 14 days, then stop.
- Do not use this cream on the face, groin, or armpits.
- Apply a fragrance-free moisturizer at least twice daily, including after bathing.
- Use lukewarm showers and fragrance-free soap.

When to seek care:
- Oozing, yellow crusting, pus, rapidly spreading redness, or fever: book a visit the same day, as this may be an infection.
- No improvement after 14 days: book a follow-up visit.
